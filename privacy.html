Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

The short version

neverloseawebsite stores your saved websites only on your own device. We do not collect, send, sell or share your data.

What the extension saves

When you tap Lock in Memory, the extension saves the page address, title, short page description, your note, your tags and your folder name. This is stored in your browser using Chrome's local storage. It never leaves your device and we cannot see it.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising inside the extension.
  • No sending your data to any server.
  • Tags are suggested on your device by reading the page you are on, using simple keyword rules. No page content is sent to any AI service.

Why the extension needs permissions

  • storage: to keep your saved websites and folders on your device.
  • activeTab: to read the title and address of the page you are on, only when you click the icon.
  • scripting: to read the page's meta description, meta keywords and main headings, only when you click the icon, so tags that match the page can be suggested. This text is used on your device and is never sent anywhere.

This website

The website neverloseawebsite.me uses Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the pages, such as clicks and scrolling. Clarity may use cookies. This applies only to the website. It does not apply to the extension, and no websites you save in the extension are ever shared with Clarity. You can learn more in Microsoft's privacy statement.

Deleting your data

You can delete any saved website inside the extension. Removing the extension from Chrome deletes all saved data.
